Understanding CIBIL Scores

Poor (300-549)

Indicates a history of payment delays or defaults. Loan approval is unlikely without significant collateral or guarantor.

Average (550-649)

Shows some credit risk. Approval may be possible but with higher interest rates and stricter conditions.

Good (650-749)

Indicates responsible credit management. Most lenders will approve loans at competitive rates.

Excellent (750-900)

Demonstrates excellent credit history. Access to best interest rates and loan products.

How to Improve Your CIBIL Score:

  • Pay all bills and EMIs on time
  • Keep credit utilization below 30% of limit
  • Don't apply for too many loans simultaneously
  • Maintain a mix of credit types (card, loan, etc.)
  • Regularly check your credit report for errors
  • Avoid defaulting on any payment obligations

Co-Applicant Information

For education loans, a co-applicant (usually a parent or guardian) is required to strengthen the application. Their income is considered along with the student's income for eligibility assessment.

Why Co-Applicant?

  • Provides additional financial stability
  • Shares the repayment responsibility
  • Improves approval chances
  • Common requirement for student loans
