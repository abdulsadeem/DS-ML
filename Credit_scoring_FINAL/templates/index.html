Understanding Our Eligibility Criteria

Our AI-powered system uses CIBIL score and EMI capacity for decisions

✓ Approved

Low Risk Profile

  • CIBIL Score: ≥ 750
  • EMI Burden: ≤ 50% of monthly income
  • Decision: Loan Approved
⚡ Conditional

Medium Risk Profile

  • CIBIL Score: 650-749
  • EMI Burden: ≤ 50% of monthly income
  • Decision: May Approve with verification
✗ Rejected

High Risk Profile

  • CIBIL Score: < 650
  • EMI Burden: > 50% of monthly income
  • Decision: Not Eligible
