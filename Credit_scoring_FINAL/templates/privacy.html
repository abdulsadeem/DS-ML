Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Smart Lending ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services. By accessing Smart Lending, you acknowledge that you have read and understand this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information from you in several ways:

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Registration: Full name, email address, phone number, username, password
  • Financial Information: Income, employment details, CIBIL score, loan history, co-applicant information
  • Contact Details: Address, location, communication preferences
  • Application Data: Loan type, amount requested, tenure, purpose

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns, referral source
  • IP Address: Your Internet Protocol address for security and analytics
  • Cookies and Tracking: Session identifiers and preferences

2.3 Third-Party Data

  • CIBIL Information: Credit scores and reports from credit bureaus
  • Verification Services: Employment and income verification data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide loan eligibility assessments and recommendations
  • Account Management: To create, maintain, and manage your account
  • Communication: To send service updates, support responses, and promotions
  • Verification: To verify your identity and prevent fraud
  • Algorithm Improvement: To enhance our assessment algorithms (using anonymized data)
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with laws, regulations, and court orders
  • Analytics: To understand user behavior and improve our platform
  • Risk Assessment: To evaluate creditworthiness and loan risk

4. Data Security

Smart Lending implements industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Secure Storage: AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Access Control: Role-based access to sensitive information
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and penetration testing
  • Password Security: Bcrypt hashing for password storage
  • Firewall Protection: Multi-layer firewall infrastructure

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Financial Institutions: Banks and NBFCs for loan processing (with your consent)
  • Credit Bureaus: For credit verification and reporting
  • Service Providers: Third parties assisting with operations (email, hosting, analytics)
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or court order
  • Insurance Partners: For loan insurance offerings (optional)

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Any sharing is done with appropriate confidentiality agreements.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Right to Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Opt-out of specific data processing activities
  • Right to Lodge Complaints: File complaints with data protection authorities

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@smartlending.in with "Data Subject Request" in the subject line.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Smart Lending uses cookies to:

  • Maintain your session and login status
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze website usage patterns
  • Prevent fraud and enhance security

You can control cookie settings in your browser. Disabling cookies may affect platform functionality.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information based on:

  • Active Accounts: While your account is active and for 1 year after closure
  • Legal Requirements: As mandated by applicable laws (minimum 7 years for financial records)
  • Dispute Resolution: Until disputes are resolved
  • Historical Data: Anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for analytics

9. Children's Privacy

Smart Lending does not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18 years of age. Our services are intended for adults. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will delete it immediately. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided information to us should contact us immediately.

10. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for:

  • Privacy practices of linked websites
  • Content and policies of third-party services
  • Security of external platforms

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services before providing information.

11. International Data Transfers

Your information is primarily stored and processed in India. If your data is transferred internationally, we ensure it meets applicable data protection standards through appropriate legal mechanisms such as data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses.

12. Security Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that compromises your personal information, we will:

  • Notify you without unreasonable delay (maximum 72 hours)
  • Provide details of the breach and affected data
  • Offer recommendations to protect your account
  • Provide credit monitoring services if applicable

13. Updates to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes via email. Your continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of updated policies.

14. CIBIL Data Handling

As authorized users of credit bureau data:

  • We handle CIBIL information as per CIBIL guidelines and regulations
  • Data is used exclusively for loan eligibility assessment
  • Your CIBIL report is not shared with other borrowers
  • Multiple credit inquiries within 30 days are counted as one inquiry

15. Your Responsibilities

To help us protect your privacy:

  • Keep your login credentials confidential
  • Change your password regularly
  • Notify us of unauthorized account access
  • Provide accurate and truthful information
  • Use secure networks when accessing your account
